The format

A business showcase
built as a game show.

This is not a concert. It is a live game and music show where the whole hall plays — and where the businesses of the city are presented on the main screen, named from the stage, and put into the prize rounds. Fast, loud, and finished in under three hours.

01

Businesses on the big screen

Every partner gets a slide, a presentation or a film on the main LED, plus a live introduction from the host — name, business, and what they do. Nobody has to walk past a stand to find them.

02

Games, quizzes & prizes

Audience selection, speed rounds, the family challenge, the Business Battle, the Golden Box and the BK Jackpot — back to back across three hours, nothing over twenty minutes, a prize given away in the first five.

03

Everybody plays

Every ticket carries a BK Player ID. The system pulls names at random through the night, and the whole hall plays the quiz rounds from their seats on their phones.

Your host
